About

Biography

I am a research supervisor at the Pediatric Research Institute of Guangzhou Women and Children's Medical Center. My work sits at the intersection of medical bioinformatics, single-cell genomics, and pediatric hepatobiliary diseases.

Trained at Peking University with a Ph.D. in Medical Bioinformatics under the mentorship of Prof. Yuan Zhou, I develop and apply computational methods to decode the molecular mechanisms underlying neonatal diseases, with a primary focus on biliary atresia and immune dysregulation.

我现任广州市妇女儿童医疗中心儿科研究所研究主管，毕业于北京大学基础医学专业（学士）及医学生物信息学专业（博士）。 研究方向聚焦于单细胞转录组学、生物信息学方法开发以及新生儿肝胆疾病免疫机制。

Research Focus

🧬

Single-Cell Computational Methods

Developing graph embedding-based imputation tools (GE-Impute) and spatial transcriptome mapping algorithms (Celloc) to enhance the resolution and accuracy of single-cell omics data analysis.

开发基于图嵌入的单细胞填补方法 (GE-Impute) 和空间转录组映射算法 (Celloc)， 提升单细胞组学数据分析的分辨率和准确性。

🫁

Neonatal Hepatobiliary Immunology

Investigating the role of CD14⁺CD16⁻ monocytes and NF-κB hyperactivation in biliary atresia pathogenesis, combining single-cell profiling with murine therapeutic validation to uncover actionable immune targets.

利用单细胞转录组学技术研究 CD14⁺CD16⁻ 单核细胞及其 NF-κB 通路过度激活在胆道闭锁发病机制中的作用， 结合小鼠模型进行治疗验证。

🫀

Disease Transcriptomics & Deconvolution

Reconstructing cell-type-specific expression signatures from bulk transcriptomes across cardiac diseases and hypertension, revealing immune cell subpopulation dynamics in cardiovascular pathology.

从 bulk 转录组数据中重构细胞类型特异性表达特征，揭示心血管疾病中免疫细胞亚群的动态变化及其功能意义。

💻

Post-Transcriptional Regulation

Computational screening of regulators governing mRNA-protein expression discordance; development of GlyinsRNA — a web server for predicting glycosylation sites on small RNAs.

计算筛选 mRNA-蛋白表达差异的调控因子；开发 GlyinsRNA 在线工具用于预测小 RNA 糖基化位点。
